Subject: ALLOCATION FOR LOCALLY-ADMINISTERED ACTIVE TRANSPORTATION
         PROGRAM PROJECTS OFF THE STATE HIGHWAY SYSTEM
         RESOLUTION FATP-2425-17

ISSUE:
Should the California Transportation Commission (Commission) approve an allocation of
$99,089,000 for 22 locally-administered Active Transportation Program (ATP) projects, off the
State Highway System?
RECOMMENDATION:
The California Department of Transportation recommends that the Commission approve an
allocation of $99,089,000 for 22 locally-administered ATP projects, off the State Highway
System.
BACKGROUND:
The attached vote list describes 22 locally-administered ATP projects totaling $99,089,000.
The local agencies are ready to proceed with these projects, and are requesting an allocation
at this time.
FINANCIAL RESOLUTION:
Resolved, that $99,089,000 be allocated from the Budget Acts of 2023 and 2024, Budget Act
Items 2660-108-0890, 2660-108-3290 and Non-Budget Act Item 2660-608-0001 for 22
locally-administered ATP projects described on the attached vote list.
