Subject: ALLOCATION FOR LOCALLY-ADMINISTERED ACTIVE TRANSPORTATION
         PROGRAM PROJECTS OFF THE STATE HIGHWAY SYSTEM
         RESOLUTION FATP-2223-10

ISSUE:

Should the California Transportation Commission (Commission) approve an allocation of
$9,261,000 for eight locally-administered Active Transportation Program (ATP) projects, off the
State Highway System?
RECOMMENDATION:

The California Department of Transportation recommends that the Commission approve an
allocation of $9,261,000 for eight locally-administered ATP projects, off the State Highway
System.
BACKGROUND:

The attached vote list describes eight locally-administered ATP projects totaling $9,261,000.
The local agencies are ready to proceed with these projects, and are requesting an allocation
at this time.
FINANCIAL RESOLUTION:

Resolved that $9,261,000 be allocated from the Budget Act of 2021 and 2022, Budget Act
Items 2660-102-0001, and 2660-108-0890 for eight locally-administered ATP projects
described on the attached vote list.
