Subject: ALLOCATION FOR ACTIVE TRANSPORTATION PROGRAM PROJECTS
         RESOLUTION FATP-1819-14

ISSUE:
Should the California Transportation Commission (Commission) approve an allocation of
$68,096,000 for 57 Active Transportation Program (ATP) projects?
RECOMMENDATION:
The California Department of Transportation recommends that the Commission approve an
allocation of $68,096,000 for 57 ATP projects as follows:
      2.5w.(1a) - $52,695,000 for 36 ATP projects and
      2.5w.(1b) - $15,401,000 for 21 ATP (SB1 Augmentation) projects.

BACKGROUND:
The attached vote list describes 57 ATP projects totaling $68,096,000. The local agencies are
ready to proceed with these projects and are requesting an allocation at this time.
FINANCIAL RESOLUTION:
Resolved that $68,096,000 be allocated from the Budget Act of 2017 and 2018, Budget Act
Items 2660-108-0042, 2660-108-0890 and 2660-108-3290 for 57 ATP projects described on
the attached vote list.
