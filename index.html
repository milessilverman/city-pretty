    How accurate is this? What has and has not been checked
    
      
    
        No accuracy percentage is shown for any part of City Pretty, because none has been measured. Below is what each part is built from and how far it has been checked (audit of 16 Nov 2026; details in docs/audit/ in the repository).
      
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Parcel lines
          From county services
        
    
        
    Lot polygons and parcel numbers come live from county assessor or GIS services where a county publishes them. Not independently re-surveyed.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          State housing laws
          Text checked for most
        
    
        
    Read from app/data/statutes.json. Each law shows whether its text was checked; fields that could not be confirmed are left out. Screening only: confirm with the planning department.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Flood zones
          From FEMA
        
    
        
    FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, queried live. No polygon means FEMA has no effective map there, not that there is no risk.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Building outlines and stories
          Not measured
        
    
        
    OpenStreetMap outlines where mapped; story count from records or set by hand. Imagery capture dates are not on file.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Transit corridors
          Partly checked
        
    
        
    A few planned corridors with agency links. Some station locations are illustrative and are labeled unverified.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Home value estimate
          Not tested
        
    
        
    Built from a recent sale, the assessor's value and the Census median. It has never been tested against recorded sales, so no accuracy range is given.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Rents
          From HUD
        
    
        
    HUD Fair Market Rents for every county, pulled from HUD's published service. These are voucher payment benchmarks, not market asking rents.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Research studies
          Citations checked
        
    
        
    Each study shows whether its citation was confirmed (Crossref or the publisher). Effect sizes are shown only where confirmed in the source.
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
        Costs, returns, cash flow and development margins are not modeled in most places yet: where no sourced cost data exists, the page says "Not modeled yet" instead of showing a number.

    Methodology & Dataset Vintages
    
    
    City Pretty estimates how much home value and quality of life each public improvement would add in every Census block group of Los Angeles County, and what it would cost.
    
    
    
    Return per $1 and which estimates are shown
    
    
    Each project's return is the home value it is modeled to add to nearby homes, divided by its lifecycle cost (construction plus ten years of discounted upkeep). A dollar figure is shown only for projects whose effect size or cost was checked against its source in the 16 November 2026 data audit (labelled Verified or Partly verified, with the unchecked side named). Every other project is listed under "Other ideas (effect not sourced yet)" with no numbers. The range shown around an estimate is a judgment band by the catalog's own evidence grade (about ±8% to ±25%); it is not a statistical confidence interval. Neighborhood "value created" totals are not modeled yet.
    
    
    No local adjustment: an earlier "locally context-adjusted" return re-ranked projects with hand-typed factors for heat, canopy, bus frequency, dining, freeway distance and a saturation curve. None had a source, so it was removed on 17 November 2026; every return shown is the catalog's own estimate with its judgment band.
