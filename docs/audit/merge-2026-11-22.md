## Which exclusion-engine design was kept, and why

Both sides replaced the blanket site rule with per-law conditions, independently:

| | Trunk (da08060, 5aae840, f1eca74, 3715588) | Other (44db697, 0dce978, 5d3f5bc) |
|---|---|---|
| Data | `exclusion_sources` per law: key, statute wording, citation, URL, date; `effect: "limits"` for a narrowing; `exclusions_review.status` confirmed / not confirmed | `site_conditions` per law for five page flags: `excluded` true / false / "conditional" / null, quote, URL; plus the old `statutory_exclusions` strings |
| Unchecked flag (null or left out) | listed item not checked: "not determined" | only a null FEMA or fire flag held a law back; an unchecked farmland or land flag, or any flag left out, counted as "no" |
| List never checked against the text | "not determined" | the law applied unless a hazard flag was true |
| Items the page never measures (airport zones, wetlands, easements...) | listed; "not determined" until checked | not modelled (only five flags), so a law with such items applied |
| A flag the law does not list | no effect | a true hazard flag the text "does not settle" held the law back |

**Kept: the trunk's design**, because it never says "allowed" on an unconfirmed list or an unchecked flag, a flag the
law does not list has no effect, and every output quotes the statute's own words. The other branch's design said
"applies" for SB 9 once three of its ten site items answered "no", and for any law whose text was never checked.

**Ported from the other side onto it:**
- its "conditional" kind (an exclusion only on terms the page cannot see: a local ordinance or determination) as a new
  entry `effect: "conditional"`: present or not checked, "not determined" with the terms and the statute quoted; never a
  ruling-out;
- the text's exceptions (`exception` on an entry, quoted with a ruling-out);
- its per-condition evidence, kept on each record as `site_conditions` (not read by the engine);
- the LA zoning-code fix, `zoningCategory` / `bundleZoneClass` (the zoning map's class decides), `is_multifamily_residential`,
  the ADU law's `applies_to_zone: "residential"` (single-family or multifamily zoning), the Santa Clarita SB 9 zones, the
  playbook's "Not confirmed" / "Ruled out here" lines (now fed by `excluded_by_statute`), and the auditor's
  `not_confirmed` / `excluded_by_statute` outputs;
- kept from the trunk: the Arizona ADU exclusions (A.R.S. 9-461.18(G)), the page passing unchecked flags as null, and
  `scripts/live_check_exclusions.mjs`.

Both engine copies (`app/app.js`, `src/engines/statutoryZoningEngine.js`, and `auditSingleHouseParcel`) are the same
text (`scripts/check_engine_copies.mjs`), and `tests/unit/engineCopies.test.ts` now also runs the page's copy, cut out of
`app/app.js`, against the tested copy on 1,000+ lots (every state with a law; three zone classes; flood, fire and
historic each true, false and not checked; other listed items absent, then present) and requires the same output, with
each outcome (applied, excluded, conditional, not checked) reached.

`app/data/statutes.json` and `app/data/state-housing-laws.json` stay byte-identical (both sides kept them so; the page
reads `statutes.json`, tests read both).
