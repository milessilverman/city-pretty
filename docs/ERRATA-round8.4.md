Each finding was checked by two reviewers who tried to refute it, and two more were refuted. The audit's evidence is in `logs/audit85_result.json`. Findings 7 and 8 concern round 8.5's merge (metrics_oc v4, installed at 09:52, after the 09:50 delivery) and were measured on its re-runs and on batch 6. Finding 15's 265 was counted after those re-runs (288 at delivery: finding 33). Each entry gives where the problem is and what is wrong (the finder's words), the two reviewers' verdicts and amendments (they take precedence where they differ from the finder), and what round 8.5 did. The 41 entries filed under the report's sections also give the corrected text; findings 9, 10, 12 and 15, filed under code, point to the entries that hold theirs. The round-8.5 report cites these findings by their numbers here. Times are 8 Nov 2026 unless a date is given (finding 43's 22:19 and 22:21 are 7 Nov).

## Code: weekday selection (fetch_oc.py)

### 1. Two Wednesdays with zero departures outvote a real schedule, and selection 16 then drops the whole feed as 'no service near today' (high)

- **Where:** _choose_wednesday lines 408-411 (within / shared / ref); consequence in _gtfs_feed lines 478-485 (elif not svc branch) and fetch_gtfs_mdb lines 744-745, 751-753
- **Problem:** `within(0, 0)` is True (0.9*0 <= 0 <= 0/0.9), so any two candidates with 0 departures form a 'shared' level of 0. If the candidates that do have service are not within 10% of each other (a single one, or an unequal pair like the docstring's own Greater Portland 3,066 / 2,744), `ref = max(shared)` is 0. The chosen day is then the nearest Wednesday with no departures. When that day has no active service, the selection-16 branch records the feed as skipped with a false reason ('no service on the Wednesdays within three weeks of today'). It returns a result with no stops, and fetch_gtfs_mdb treats that as final: no other copy and no other candidate of the provider is tried. The operator's buses are lost for the county. Selection 14 introduced the tie; the busiest-Wednesday fallback hid it in 14/15; selection 16 turned it into a dropped feed. It also contradicts REPORT line 178: 'the busier schedule is measured, whether it is the coming one or the one before'.
- **Reviewer 1:** Confirmed as a latent defect. No delivered round-8.4 or batch-6 feed is affected today. 'Both pass only because their service days are equal' is exact for Lewis (two non-zero days). PART (three days of 415) would still pass if any two of its three days stayed within 10%.
- **Reviewer 2:** Latent: no delivered or batch-6 county is affected yet. PART (three equal non-zero days) fails only if all three differ pairwise by more than 10%. A Lewis-type feed also fails with just one non-zero candidate, i.e. a new schedule starting within the window. The finding's 'high' severity is about future builds, not delivered data.
- **Round 8.5:** Fixed in transit selection 17 (13:23): Wednesdays without departures form no level of service. One built feed had this case: Home James's Winter Park Shuttle (MobilityData 607), which Adams, Denver and Jefferson, CO measured at selection 16 on 4 Nov (no departures 07:00–09:00) instead of 25 Nov. Adams and Denver were re-run at selection 17 (25 Nov, its Denver Airport stop added); no bus value changed.

### 2. The selection's 07:00-09:00 count ignores frequencies.txt: headway-based feeds record 0 departures on every Wednesday and the weekday rules cannot act on them (medium)

- **Where:** _gtfs_feed lines 470-477 (per_sid / ntrips), against the measurement at lines 498-501 and 516-520
- **Problem:** The comment at l.470 says the count is 'the quantity measured'. In fact per_sid counts a stop_times row only when its literal time is between 07:00:00 and 09:00:00. Trips defined in frequencies.txt (template times such as 00:00:00 plus headways) count 0, while the measurement expands them. Three consequences: (1) wednesdays_7_9am_departures in the meta is wrong for these feeds. REPORT line 100 presents these counts as each feed's evidence ('Each feed's candidate counts are now in the county's transit meta'). (2) All candidates tie at 0, so the holiday-level, feed-error and lapse rules cannot work, and the choice degenerates to the nearest non-holiday Wednesday. (3) Selection 16's rule at l.406 ('nearest Wednesday that has departures 07:00-09:00') never matches, so a future or ended headway-based copy is judged around a day without service. When that day is empty, the fallback `max(weds, key=ntrips)` returns the calendar's first Wednesday, because every count ties at 0.
- **Reviewer 1:** As stated, with two small detail fixes. Clovis template times are 00:00:00-01:05:00 or blank, not only '01:00:00'. JAC headways run 2940-3220 s, with a second window of 08:15-16:45. Dana Point's zeros are correct, since it runs 10:00-22:00. The effect is wrong evidence counts and inoperative rules for headway feeds; no delivered bus value changes for the cited feeds.
- **Reviewer 2:** Two evidence details are imprecise. Clovis template times are not only '01:00:00' or blank; there are also 00:00:00, 00:06:00 and 00:09:00. JAC headways run 2940-3220 s, and one window is 08:15-16:45. Mixed feeds are affected too: Mountain Line 407 in 54061 records 337 against 751 measured. No chosen day changes in delivered data.
- **Round 8.5:** Fixed in transit selection 18 (16:03): headway trips are counted in the weekday comparison. In a scratch run of Fresno at selection 18, Clovis Transit records 215 departures per Wednesday instead of 0, no chosen day changes and Fresno's 3,326 stops are identical.
